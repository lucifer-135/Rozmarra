Step 4 · A trade-off

Sunita fasts without food or water.

Sunita Sharma keeps Karva Chauth. Dr Ananya tests a changed plan:

  • Glimepiride: 2 mg at 8 am becomes 1 mg at 4:30 am
  • Dapagliflozin: 10 mg at 8 am becomes 10 mg at 8:30 pm
Chance of low blood sugar during the fast4% → 1% Chance of high blood sugar after the fast80% → 86%

The changed plan lowers one risk and raises another. Rozmarra shows the trade-off. It does not hide it.

Dehydration caution. This fast allows no water for 15.2 hours. The risk of dehydration may be higher for this patient. Please review the medicines and the plan for drinking water before and after the fast. The patient takes dapagliflozin, an SGLT2 inhibitor.

Step 5 · The family plan

After approval, the family gets a clear plan.

The plan comes from fixed, written templates in English and Hindi. The rule to break the fast comes first. Approved by Dr Ananya Rao on 12 November 2026.

Your fasting plan for Karva Chauth on 28 October 2026

Sunita Sharma, Age 54

The most important rule Please break your fast immediately if your blood sugar is below 70 mg/dL or above 300 mg/dL, or if you feel unwell.

Your fast

Your fast (व्रत) will start at 5 am and will end at 8:14 pm.

This fast does not allow water. Please drink enough water before the fast starts and after it ends.

When to check your blood sugar

Please check your blood sugar at 7 am, 10 am, 1 pm, 4 pm, 7 pm and 10:30 pm.

Please also check your blood sugar at any time if you feel unwell.

Sweating, shaking, a fast heartbeat, strong hunger and confusion can be signs of low blood sugar.

Your medicines

  • Your doctor has advised you to take Glimepiride 1 mg at 4:30 am, instead of 2 mg at 8 am.
  • Please take Metformin 500 mg at 8 am as usual.
  • Your doctor has advised you to take Dapagliflozin 10 mg at 8:30 pm, instead of at 8 am.
  • Please take Metformin 500 mg at 8:30 pm as usual.

Your meals

  • 4:30 am: Sargi before sunrise, with about 60 g of carbohydrate.
  • 8:24 pm: Meal that breaks the fast, with water and sweets, with about 110 g of carbohydrate.

Your fast will end with the meal at 8:24 pm. Please start this meal with water and a small portion of food. Please eat sweets only in a small amount.

Your doctor

If you have any questions about this plan, please contact Dr Ananya Rao.

Approved by Dr Ananya Rao on 12 November 2026

Safe by design

The doctor decides.

  • Every sentence a patient reads comes from fixed, written templates. Rozmarra does not use AI to write medical advice.
  • Rozmarra never changes a medicine on its own.
  • Rozmarra never judges the decision to fast.

The evidence

How well does Rozmarra predict low blood sugar?

  • 94 in 100Rozmarra picks the hour that ends in low blood sugar.Real data: normal days of 89 patients.
  • 97 in 100Rozmarra finds the patient at risk before the fast.Simulated: 1,500 fasting days.
  • 88 in 100Rozmarra catches low blood sugar events in time.Simulated: 1,500 fasting days.
  • 25 minutesThis is the typical warning before low blood sugar.Simulated: 1,500 fasting days.

“94 in 100” means that, when Rozmarra compares an hour that ended in low blood sugar with one that did not, it gives the risky hour the higher chance 94 times in 100. Random guessing would get 50. It is not accuracy. Technical measures: AUROC 0.943 and 0.973.

Rozmarra is a research prototype. It is not an approved medical device, and no doctor has reviewed its medical content yet.
